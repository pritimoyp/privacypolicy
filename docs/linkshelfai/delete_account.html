You can delete your account data at any time. LinkShelfAI stores data in two places — follow the steps below for each.

1

Local App Data

Automatic

Uninstall the app

All locally stored data — links, folders, and profile info — is permanently deleted when you uninstall LinkShelfAI from your device. No additional steps needed.

2

Google Drive Backup Data

Manual — 3 steps

Delete hidden app data from Google Drive

If you used the backup feature, a private backup file is stored in your Google Drive App Data folder. Delete it by following these steps:

1

Open Google Drive on your phone or browser.

2

Tap the Settings (gear icon) → Manage apps.

3

Find LinkShelfAI → tap Delete hidden app data → confirm.

This folder is private and accessible only by you. The developer cannot view or access your backup data.
